# Olist E-commerce Data Analysis Project

## Project Overview
This project explores the Olist Brazilian E-commerce dataset to evaluate business performance, customer purchasing behaviour, delivery efficiency, and seller performance.

The analysis uses SQL for data exploration and querying, and Microsoft Power BI for data modelling, visualization, and reporting. The goal is to transform raw e-commerce data into actionable business insights that can support better decision-making.

## Business Objectives
- Evaluate overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour and repeat purchase rates.
- Assess delivery performance and late delivery patterns.
- Examine seller performance and operational efficiency.
- Identify opportunities to improve customer satisfaction.

## Tools and Technologies
- **SQL (MySQL):** Data cleaning, exploration, aggregation, and analysis.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Data modelling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboards.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data inspection and validation.

## Project Dashboards

The Power BI report contains four interactive dashboard pages:

1. **Executive Overview:** Overall business performance and key performance indicators.
2. **Operations:** Delivery performance and operational efficiency.
3. **Customers & Sales:** Customer behaviour, sales trends, and product category performance.
4. **Seller Details:** Seller performance and distribution insights.

## Key Findings
- Health & Beauty was the highest-revenue product category.
- Late deliveries accounted for approximately 7.87% of orders.
- Customers who received orders on time gave an average review score of 4.21, compared with 2.57 for late deliveries.
- Repeat purchasing was approximately 3.12%, indicating an opportunity to improve customer retention.
